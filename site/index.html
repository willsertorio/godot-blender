The game

No two runs share a layout

Each run generates a dungeon of 8 to 12 rooms joined by corridors and doors, then places robots, loot crates and one exit portal through it. Three robots hunt you: a four-legged spider that walks the dungeon, a drone that chases and explodes when shot down, and a ceiling turret that winds up and sweeps the room.

Score comes only from kills. Reach the portal and the next dungeon has more robots, tougher ones, and fewer med kits; fall, and you start again at the same threat level.
